# CareCompass Clinical Taxonomy & Classification Standards

## Care Types
1. **paramedic_home_visit**: Acute or subacute symptoms (moderate fever, chills, dehydration, general malaise) where vital signs, oxygen saturation, blood sugar, and rapid clinical examination need in-person measurement at home.
2. **nurse_home_visit**: Patients requiring procedural nursing care (wound dressings, suture removal, IV/IM injections, catheter flushes, palliative bedridden care, bedsore management).
3. **gp_teleconsult**: Non-urgent complaints, mild seasonal colds, routine medication refills, early-stage symptoms, and general medical advice manageable remotely.
4. **specialist**: Chronic or organ-specific complaints without immediate life threats (chronic back pain -> orthopaedics, persistent rash -> dermatology, chronic migraine -> neurology).
5. **emergency_referral**: Acute life-threatening conditions (chest pain, stroke signs, unconsciousness, severe respiratory failure, heavy hemorrhage, poisoning). Immediate transport to the nearest emergency facility.

## Urgency Levels
- **routine**: Can be managed within 2-5 days without clinical deterioration.
- **same_day**: Requires medical evaluation or home visit within 12-24 hours to prevent complications.
- **immediate**: Requires emergency intervention within minutes (<30 minutes); call 112/102 immediately.
